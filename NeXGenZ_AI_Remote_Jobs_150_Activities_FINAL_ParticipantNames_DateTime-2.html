FAQ

Are all 150 opportunities currently hiring?
No. The catalogue contains possible AI remote job and task types. “Available” and “Limited” are catalogue labels and do not guarantee a live vacancy.
What are the available spaces?
The displayed 20–100 spaces are catalogue availability figures used by this NeXGenZ interface. They should not be treated as confirmed provider vacancy counts.
Do I need experience?
It depends on the specialization. Many data, evaluation, search, audio, image, and content tasks can be beginner friendly, while language, technical, and specialist tasks may require stronger skills.
How do I apply?
Review the opportunity details, then follow the relevant provider's application process. This may include creating an account, completing a profile, passing a qualification test, completing training, and waiting for approval.
Can I save opportunities?
Yes. Use the Save function on an opportunity. Saved items are stored in your browser using local storage.
Is payment guaranteed?
No. Payment, eligibility, task availability, rates, and provider terms vary by opportunity. Always check the actual provider's current terms before applying or working.
